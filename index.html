This policy describes how ChatHub (application ID app.chathub.android) handles information when you use its Android app. ChatHub connects to AI providers and compatible endpoints that you choose.

Information on your device

  • Conversations: prompts, responses, and conversation metadata support chat history and conversational context.
  • Attachments: selected images and supported files may be copied into private app storage for use with your conversations.
  • Preferences and saved content: model choices, saved prompts, saved responses, and personalization memory support the features you enable.
  • Credentials: API keys and endpoint credentials are stored using encrypted preferences backed by Android Keystore and used to authenticate requests.

The current app does not require a ChatHub account and does not integrate an advertising or analytics service. Android backup and device-transfer rules exclude app data.

Information sent to AI providers

When you request a response, ChatHub sends the prompt, relevant conversation context, supported attachment content, and enabled personalization information to the provider or endpoint selected for that request. Multi-model requests send relevant content to each selected provider. Regenerating with another provider sends the context needed for that request to that provider. Credentials are used for authentication.

Providers process requests under their own terms, privacy policies, retention rules, and account settings. ChatHub cannot delete their copies by deleting data on your phone. Review the selected provider's policy before sending personal or sensitive information.

Recipients of network requests can see connection information such as your IP address. Cloud and Play-build endpoint connections use HTTPS. A separately distributed sideload build may allow HTTP connections to user-configured local endpoints after a warning; those connections are not encrypted in transit.

Web content and external links

Retrieving web context, displaying source favicons, or opening a link can contact the relevant website. Those websites receive ordinary connection information and apply their own privacy practices. Temporary web content and favicons may be cached locally.

Reporting generated content

The report feature is designed to send a report only after you review and submit it. Its payload includes the category, optional comment, selected response (up to 12,000 characters), app version, distribution channel, Android version, and device manufacturer and model. It does not separately attach your prompt, surrounding chat history, attachments, credentials, advertising ID, or a persistent device identifier. Information quoted within the selected response or typed into your comment is included.

Draft item: reporting is not operational in the reviewed configuration. Identify the receiving service, permitted access, purposes, retention period, infrastructure logging, and deletion process before enabling reports and publishing the final policy.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

You can delete conversations and use Privacy & data to delete chats, memory, provider keys, or all local app data. Saved content and settings can also be removed by clearing all local app data. Local information remains until removed through the relevant controls or Android's app-data controls; cached files may be cleared sooner.

Exported or shared files can remain in Downloads or in the destination you selected. Deleting ChatHub data does not delete those copies or data retained by AI providers. Use the destination's controls to manage them. Removing a stored key does not revoke it at the provider; use the provider's account settings to revoke it.

Security

ChatHub uses private app storage, encrypted credential storage, and HTTPS for cloud requests. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Only send information you intend the selected service to process.

Age suitability

ChatHub is intended for adults aged 18 or older and asks for age confirmation during onboarding.
